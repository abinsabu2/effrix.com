Get started

Log in with Telegram

  1. 1

    Enter your Telegram phone number (do not include the "+" sign).

  2. 2

    Check your Telegram app — you will receive an authentication code on your phone or logged-in device.

  3. 3

    Enter the code in gPlayer to complete the login.

  4. 4

    You're now connected — your Telegram media will be available on your TV.

About Telegram

Telegram is a widely used messaging platform known for its speed, simplicity, and strong security. With cloud-based storage and privacy-focused features, it lets you keep messages and media accessible across devices. gPlayer builds on that foundation by surfacing your existing Telegram media library — chats, saved messages, and personal files — on the big screen for a comfortable TV viewing experience.

About Effrix & gPlayer privacy

gPlayer is an independent software tool and personal Telegram media player created for Smart TV platforms. The app connects strictly via Telegram's official TDLib API, allowing authorized users to access files, videos, and media stored within their personal account saved chats, channels, or groups.

Effrix and gPlayer do not host, store, stream, or distribute unauthorized third-party media. The app does not download content from unauthorized internet sources.
